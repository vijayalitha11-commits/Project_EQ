What is an earthquake?

  • An earthquake is the shaking of the Earth's surface resulting from a sudden release of energy in the lithosphere that creates seismic waves.
  • Seismic magnitude scales are used to describe the overall strength or "size" of an earthquake.
  • The mortality of an earthquake can vary greatly depending on many factors (i.e. magnitude, location, infrastructure quality).

Results and discussion

Hypothesis

At first, our hypothesis was that with time, earthquakes have less impact on mortality, as society got better at building antisismic buildings.

Results

With the graph, we see that the mortality caused by earthquakes is not correlated with time.

Discussion

This could be explained by the difficulty to prevent earthquakes. Indeed, most of the time they are only detected a few moments before they occur.
Moreover, the mortality could be higher in developing countries that cannot invest in antisismic buildings.
